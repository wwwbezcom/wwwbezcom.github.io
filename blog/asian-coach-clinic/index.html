Asian Coach Clinic

The Asian Coach Clinic brought a group of accomplished Asian basketball coaches to the United States for an exchange focused on coaching practice, player development, and high-performance training. The program began in Los Angeles, where the delegation attended NBA All-Star events at Intuit Dome, before continuing to the University of Missouri. At Mizzou, the group observed team practices and studied modern five-out offense, defensive principles, and strength and conditioning methods with the Tigers’ coaching and performance staff.

Guests included UCBA representatives Enze Bai and Chairman Tracy Zhang; Chinese national-team strength and conditioning coach and basketball commentator 闫宇峰; former China U15 women’s national-team coach and CUBA Guangzhou College of Applied Science and Technology men’s basketball head coach 高科; Singapore national-team assistant coach Joe So and Indonesia National U18 Basketball Coach and SMA Jubilee Jakarta Head Coach Hoei Maechel (“Coach Mike”). The clinic concluded with a group visit to the Gateway Arch in St. Louis—a fitting final stop for a week built around learning, connection, and the shared language of basketball.
